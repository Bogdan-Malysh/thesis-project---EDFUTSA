## Family Notes

- **Baselines:** Naive, daily seasonal naive, weekly seasonal naive, and average hour-of-week profile are deterministic and have complete 2024 validation; weekly seasonal naive is the registered benchmark representative for both countries.
- **Holt-Winters:** The implementation grid contains additive/multiplicative daily and weekly seasonal variants with damped/undamped trends. The authoritative completed summary contains the shortlisted variants; unlisted grid variants are screening/experimental rather than completed full validation.
- **ARIMA:** The authoritative 2024 results contain the screened `p2_d1_q0`, `p2_d1_q1`, and `p2_d1_q2` country-specific rows; the selected specification is `arima_p2_d1_q2` for both countries.
- **SARIMA:** The authoritative full validation contains the selected and comparator specifications shown in the table; selected orders are `sarima_p2_d0_q0_P0_D1_Q0_s24` for Germany and `sarima_p1_d0_q1_P1_D1_Q0_s24` for Austria.
- **Regression with ARIMA errors:** Four calendar predictor specifications are implemented and completed for 2024. The selected `reg_arima_h_d_m_hol` rows carry residual-adequacy warnings for remaining daily autocorrelation/Ljung-Box rejection.
- **Intervention variant:** `reg_arima_h_d_m_hol_crisis` is completed for 2024 and carries the same residual-adequacy warning; COVID and post-invasion indicators are included jointly.
- **SARIMAX:** `sarimax_calendar` and `sarimax_calendar_crisis` are implemented and benchmarked/attempted, but current mini/full state-validation artifacts do not establish completed authoritative 2024 jobs. They are not selected or registered.
- **2025:** No authoritative 2025 evaluation artifact was identified.
